NRI Guidelines

NRI real estate investment made easy.

The Reserve Bank of India permits non-residents holding Indian passports, as well as people of Indian origin, to invest in residential and commercial real estate. More NRIs are investing as the rupee depreciates and property offers better returns — and a place in the homeland brings a sense of security too.

Can an NRI buy property in India?

Yes. A non-resident Indian can buy either residential or commercial property in India, and there is no limit on the number of properties an NRI can purchase.
