Overview

We take your privacy seriously. You can browse this website without telling us who you are. When you do share personal information — for example by submitting an enquiry — we use it only within i5 Housing & Properties and with trusted service providers who help us serve you.

1. Information we collect

  • Details you choose to give us, such as your name, phone number, email address or postal address.
  • Cookies and similar tracking data that help the website work properly.
  • Usage information, such as pages visited, collected through tools like Google Analytics.

2. Cookies

We use cookies to understand how the site is used and to remember information you have entered. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some features may not work as expected if you do.

3. How we use your information

When we collect personal information, we will tell you why. We use it to respond to enquiries, arrange site visits, share project information and provide after-sales support. We do not share it with third parties without your consent, except where required by law or with service providers acting on our behalf.

4. Your choices

You can decline to share personal information and still browse most of the site, although some services — such as enquiry forms — need your contact details to work. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

5. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, misuse or disclosure, both within our company and with our authorised partners.

6. Third-party services

We may share information with service providers such as payment gateways, analytics and marketing partners. They are required to protect your data and may not sell or misuse it. We will never sell your personal information.

7. Online advertising

We use Google Ads, Google remarketing and Google Analytics. These may use cookies to show you relevant ads on other websites in Google’s Display Network. We do not use sensitive personal information in ad campaigns, and we follow Google’s advertising policies. You can manage ad personalisation in your Google ad settings.

8. Children’s privacy

This website is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
