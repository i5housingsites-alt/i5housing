About i5 Housing & Properties

Every family. One dream — a place to call their own.

Since 2010, i5 Housing & Properties has helped families across Tamil Nadu own homes and land — through DTCP & RERA approved plots, villas, apartments and farm lands.

Our mission

More than land. A foundation for your future.

Our mission is to make the dream of owning a home and land a reality for every family across Tamil Nadu.

We envision a future where everyone has the opportunity to own a dream home, purchase a piece of land to build their own house, or invest in land to establish their dream business.

This is more than a movement; it is a commitment to turning aspirations into reality, creating opportunities, building financial security, and helping every family establish a place they can proudly call their own.

Founder & Managing Director

Led by vision, built on trust.

Mr. Prasad P founded i5 Housing & Properties to make quality, legally approved property affordable for ordinary families.

With a bachelor’s degree in business management and more than 18 years in real estate sales and marketing, he grew i5 from low-cost plots on the outskirts of Chennai into a multi-location brand — and today leads the i5 Group of Companies.
