What can NRIs buy in India?

We check that every property you consider falls within an NRI-eligible category.

You can buy

  • Residential plots
  • Apartments and flats
  • Villas and independent houses
  • Commercial property

Restricted

  • Agricultural land
  • Plantation property
  • Farmhouses

Restricted categories can only be acquired by inheritance or with special permission. Rules can change — confirm your eligibility with a qualified advisor.

Checklist

Documents required

  • Valid passport (with visa, OCI or PIO card if applicable)
  • PAN card
  • Address proof — Aadhaar, voter ID or proof of overseas residence
  • Passport-size photographs
  • OCI / PIO card, if applicable
RBI compliant

Payment options

  • NRE or NRO account (preferred)
  • Foreign bank transfer through SWIFT
  • Home loan from an Indian bank under an NRI scheme

All payments must follow RBI guidelines. Payments in foreign currency cash or traveller’s cheques are not accepted for property purchase.

Power of Attorney (PoA)

A PoA holder in India can sign the sale agreement, register the property and handle loan and bank formalities for you. Our legal team helps draft and register the PoA — in India, or abroad through the Indian Embassy.

Tax & compliance

TDS on sale. Tax is deducted at source when an NRI sells property in India.

Capital gains. Applies on resale, depending on how long you held the property.

Rental income. Taxable in India, with eligible deductions.

This is general information, not tax or legal advice. We can connect you with a chartered accountant or tax advisor for your situation.
